# ApplyCue

ApplyCue helps hiring teams invite candidates into clearer readiness reports.

The product supports candidate-led CV/JD gap validation, AI-assisted readiness checks, evidence-aware reports, and provider review workflows. It is designed to improve hiring conversations without replacing human judgement.

## What We Are Building

- Candidate readiness reports for specific roles
- Provider invite workflows for pre-interview preparation
- Evidence and self-attestation visibility controls
- Provider shortlists with human review signals
- Privacy-conscious AI use with clear trust boundaries

## Trust Principles

- Candidates control what they share.
- Providers use reports as decision-support, not automated hiring decisions.
- Private evidence stays private unless the candidate chooses otherwise.
- AI checks support readiness conversations; they do not determine employment outcomes.
